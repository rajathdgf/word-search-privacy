Privacy Policy

Word Search - Word Puzzle Game · Effective date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AR Foundation handles information in Word Search - Word Puzzle Game (the “App”). The App is designed for players of all ages. Please read this policy to understand what information may be processed when you use the App.

1. Information collected by the developer

AR Foundation does not ask you to create an account and does not directly collect or store personal information such as your name, email address, telephone number, or postal address through the App. The developer does not operate a server to maintain a database of players' personal information.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The App displays advertisements provided by third-party advertising services. Those providers may automatically receive or collect information from your device, such as an advertising identifier, IP address, device and operating-system information, approximate location inferred from IP address, and information about ad delivery, views, or interactions. They may use this information to provide, measure, limit, and improve advertising, subject to their own policies and settings.

AR Foundation does not control the information practices of third-party advertising providers. Please review the privacy policy of the advertising provider used in the App for details. If the App uses Google AdMob, you can read Google's information about advertising and Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Children's privacy

The App is intended for players of all ages. The developer does not knowingly request or directly collect children's personal information. Advertising services may process device or advertising information as described above. Where required, advertising settings and safeguards should be configured in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play requirements for the App's audience.

4. Data retention and security

Because the developer does not directly collect or store personal information on its own servers, the developer does not maintain a personal-information database for the App. Information processed by third-party providers is subject to their retention and security practices.

5. Your choices

You can manage advertising and privacy settings available on your Android device, including resetting or deleting the advertising ID where supported. You can also review the settings and privacy information provided by the advertising service.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the App, its services, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer through the contact information provided on the App's Google Play Store listing.
